Audit-to-repair coverage

O01 — Meaning-changing substitutions removed from legal dialogue/overlay text.
      Source-grounded checks reject unsupported mandatory claims after bounded
      repair. Visual-only formatting does not rewrite the explanation's rules.
O02 — Cards, comics and videos receive every category source quotation. Context
      accounting uses the provider tokenizer or an explicit conservative bound;
      oversized complete evidence fails explicitly rather than dropping quotes.
O03 — Extraction validates quotations against normalized source text and retains
      exact original character spans and stable evidence IDs. Fabrications fail.
O04 — Aggregation starts from valid observed severity and takes the highest
      observed level; category fallback is reserved for invalid/missing levels.
O05 — Final comic/video plans must satisfy required structure, timing and
      grounding checks. Advisory style warnings do not force expensive retries.
O06 — Original UI now follows durable video jobs and recognizes completed video
      preloads. Generic asynchronous video providers are polled to completion.
O07 — Original Workspace aborts on unmount/document changes; queued operations
      check that signal before starting and accepted late jobs are canceled.
O08 — Every Qwen stage receives cancellation and a request deadline including
      queueing and response-body reading. The API exposes cancel on all stages.
O09 — Length failures increase available output budget; extraction can split;
      invalid JSON receives specific repair feedback; transient HTTP/network
      failures use bounded retries. Context and integer settings are validated.
O10 — PDF.js is updated and eval disabled. Positioned words/lines are preserved,
      page/document resources are released, and cancellation is propagated.
      Scanned PDFs without text still require OCR; neither UI invents text.

N01 — All untrusted new-UI text is escaped and media URL schemes are checked.
N02 — Automatic and manual work share deduplicated browser queues. The server
      enforces global Qwen admission, job limits and per-model media gates.
N03 — Accepted job identity survives transient polling and submission failures.
      Submission retries reuse idempotency keys. Reload resumes stored job IDs.
N04 — Missing/failed cards and media become explicit terminal states with retry.
N05 — Complete comic collections are retained; each scenario has owned lazy
      image rendering and can be selected without losing its siblings.
N06 — One run/navigation identity invalidates stale uploads and late completions.
N07 — Cards are published per category as they settle. Media can begin while
      other cards are still running; visible work receives browser priority.
N08 — Analysis/cards/story/video cancellation propagates through every stage.
N09 — Jobs persist privately with retention, bounded record/admission counts and
      owner isolation. Restart preserves completed jobs and explicitly fails
      interrupted ones rather than silently launching duplicate generation.
N10 — A provider submission or future URL is not considered playable completion.
N11 — Canceled/expired/error states consistently stop polling and UI spinners.

M1  — Model job artifacts use unique caller-ID/UUID directories, never timestamps
      alone. Same-category concurrent inputs cannot overwrite each other.
M2  — Models require service keys and default to loopback. HTTP/body/job queues
      are bounded. Website sessions isolate job reads/writes/cancellation.
M3  — Model artifact access uses resolved containment and extension allowlists;
      sibling-prefix and symlink escapes are rejected.
M4  — Speech WAV durations determine non-overlapping subtitle slots, with bounded
      tempo adjustment and actual returned timing used by the browser.
M5  — Audio mixing uses explicit stable gain instead of amix's changing automatic
      normalization. Real ffmpeg tone regression checks cover the timeline.
M6  — HiDream reports and validates supported native dimensions and fixed steps.
      Unsupported inexpensive-looking controls fail instead of being ignored.
M7  — Video duration/fps/frame inputs are finite and bounded, and the frame count
      obeys 8k+1. Responses report actual frame-derived duration.
M8  — Job deadlines cover queueing, speech and ffmpeg as well as GPU inference.
      Speech fallback is explicit degraded output, shown by the UI.
M9  — Cancellation escalates against owned process groups even after leader exit.
M10 — HiDream retains worker ownership until its actual inference thread exits.
M11 — Resident LTX rechecks cancellation after admission and during diffusion.
M12 — Accepted job IDs remain stable through completion; filenames are separate.

I01 — undici is declared and locked (7.30.1); providers share a managed dispatcher.
I02 — Signed anonymous sessions, CSRF checks, origin allowlisting, ownership and
      admission limits protect the application boundary. Media is delivered by
      scoped signed asset URLs/authenticated proxy; diagnostics are not static.
I03 — Presentation matching requires full normalized document identity. Familiar
      filenames/prefixes cannot select another agreement. Incomplete-media
      categories remain visible; presentation mode defaults off.
I04 — Remote media URLs are preserved. An explicit origin allowlist and local
      /outputs remapping replace machine-specific filesystem assumptions.

Other reviewed weaknesses repaired
  Bounded extraction parallelism preserves global vLLM admission. Chunks include
  boundary context; merges retain later summaries, conditions and source spans.
  Source-excerpt navigation is restored in the redesigned UI. Speaker-to-voice
  mapping is stable. Environment overrides and model/repo paths are honored.
  Unneeded final LTX audio decode/encode is skipped when Kokoro replaces it;
  joint audiovisual denoising remains intact. Readiness is distinct from health.
  The required HiDream cancellation extension is versioned with a checked,
  idempotent patch helper. Both duplicate script trees match a checksum manifest.
  Original serverless API adapters proxy to the persistent backend instead of
  bypassing authentication and durable job ownership. Original dependencies and
  PDF extraction were upgraded and the production build checked.

Additional regressions caught during iterative implementation review
  Out-of-order card progress no longer overwrites earlier categories.
  Video responses with a URL plus processing/expired status do not finish early.
  Ambiguous provider transport failures request cancellation by stable job ID.
  Per-model permits are acquired before shared GPU permits, avoiding head-of-line
  occupation by two waiting image jobs while video capacity is available.
  Worker ownership uses an OS-backed SQLite lease that survives SIGKILL safely.
  Corrupt startup releases the lease; completed-job retries deduplicate even at
  the record cap. Cancellation holds retention ownership until upstream cleanup
  finishes, preventing a pruned job from being recreated on disk.
  Synchronous compatibility calls have separate idempotency namespaces so their
  response shape cannot collide with a durable job response.
  Maximum-length category IDs remain valid metadata at the image boundary.
  PDF.js 6 loading tasks are destroyed through its supported API.
  Restore handles missing/expired anonymous sessions with reupload guidance.
  Reserved scenario identifiers cannot share prototype state across categories.
  Mandatory no-document-text prompt instructions no longer trigger a contradictory
  style check and an unnecessary second full plan generation.

Activation boundary

The public UI and API must move to the new session/job contract together. Simply
restarting the API under the existing old published UI will break that client's
unauthenticated requests. Likewise, rotating a model key without its matching
backend key breaks model calls. A coordinated cutover is required.

Review and publish matching frontend assets, drain jobs and stop services during
an agreed cutover. Check the model package and HiDream patch on the deployment
host. Then run tools/prepare_runtime.py --apply while services are stopped;
it backs up changed .env files privately, creates matching strong credentials,
sets loopback service hosts, updates Qwen budgets, and selects native HiDream
size. start_eulalens.sh runs the same idempotent preparation before launch and
checks actual health/readiness. Preparation refuses configuration changes while
known service ports are listening; no live credentials were rotated here.

After activation, verify authenticated readiness and one real image/video job,
actual captions/audio, cancellation and byte-range playback. These fresh GPU
acceptance checks have not been performed against newly restarted wrappers.

Deliberate limits
  Signed anonymous sessions provide ownership, not account authentication. An
  optional access-token session gate exists for private instances, but enabling
  it requires a client/access flow; it is not configured in the public UI.
  Same-tab reload restores identity via sessionStorage. A new tab/browser with
  blocked third-party cookies may need a new upload. No source text/prompts are
  saved in browser localStorage; only opaque run/job references are retained.
  The durable job directory permits one worker process. It is not a distributed
  queue and intentionally refuses a second writer. Interrupted jobs fail clearly
  and require explicit retry; there is no claim to resume an in-flight GPU kernel.
  Cancellation acknowledgment means an upstream request was accepted, not proof
  that a GPU kernel has already stopped. Wrappers retain ownership until workers
  exit. Hardware/driver hangs remain operational failures.
  Regression/fixture tests establish control-flow and boundary behavior. Exact
  source quotation verification does not establish complete legal interpretation
  accuracy. No statistical model-quality evaluation or production-scale GPU
  throughput/tail-latency benchmark was run. Concurrency defaults are bounded
  starting settings, not a claim of optimal performance for every workload.
